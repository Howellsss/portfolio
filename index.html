About Me

Systems engineer. GoHigh Level practitioner. I fix things that are broken and build things that should exist.

I'm Howells Henry — I build systems that work and marketing funnels that convert.

My background is in systems engineering, which means I think about problems the way an engineer does: what's the input, what's the output, where's the bottleneck, and how do we make this reliable at scale.

I bring that same mindset to GoHigh Level and marketing automation. Most people set up a funnel and hope. I engineer them — triggers map to outcomes, every sequence has a purpose, and the whole thing is designed to move someone toward a decision, not just "stay in the loop."

I work with businesses that are tired of losing leads because their follow-up is manual, their systems don't talk to each other, or their funnel exists but nobody's converting through it. If that's you, we can fix it.
